About Us

House of Ranishè is a Mumbai-based jewellery brand, founded in July 2025 with a simple vision: to let women experience the beauty of gold every day, without the high cost or the constant worry of it fading.

We create durable, anti-tarnish jewellery crafted from premium stainless steel with 18K gold plating — elegant, long-lasting, and genuinely affordable. Each piece is designed to be water-resistant, hypoallergenic, and skin-safe, so you can wear it through showers, workdays, and every ordinary moment in between.

Our designs sit somewhere between minimalism and sophistication: clean silhouettes, subtle statements, and modern classics made for daily wear, work, and special occasions alike. We believe jewellery should be effortless, versatile, and timeless — pieces you reach for every day, not ones you save for a single occasion.

Every order is packed and sent with care from our home in Mumbai, across India. Thank you for being part of our story.
