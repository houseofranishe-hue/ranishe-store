Good to Know

Frequently Asked

What makes House of Ranishè different?

Most affordable jewellery is thinly plated over brass and fades within months, often leaving a green mark on your skin. Every House of Ranishè piece is built on high-quality stainless steel with genuine 18K gold plating — water-resistant, anti-tarnish, and hypoallergenic. It's real everyday luxury: pieces you can shower, sweat, and live in, that keep their shine for years, not weeks — at a price that still makes sense.

Will it tarnish or turn my skin green?

No. Because our base is stainless steel and the gold is properly plated (not a thin wash), it won't tarnish or discolour your skin the way cheap brass jewellery does.

Can I wear it in the shower or while swimming?

Yes. Every piece is water-resistant and made for daily wear — you don't need to take it off for showers, workouts, or the rain. To keep the shine at its best over years, we suggest patting it dry after heavy water exposure.

Is it safe for sensitive skin?

Yes — our pieces are hypoallergenic and nickel-free, making them comfortable for sensitive skin and everyday wear.

How do I care for my jewellery?

Very little upkeep needed. Avoid direct contact with perfumes and harsh chemicals, wipe gently with a soft cloth, and store it dry. That's it — no special pouches or polishing required.

Do you ship across India? How long does it take?

Yes, we ship pan-India from Mumbai. Orders are usually dispatched within 2–3 working days, with delivery time depending on your location. Free shipping on orders above ₹999.
