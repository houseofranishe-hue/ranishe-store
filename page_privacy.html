Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how House of Ranishè ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you visit or make a purchase from our website.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, we collect the information you provide — such as your name, phone number, delivery address, and order details — solely to process and deliver your order and to contact you about it.

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used only to fulfil your orders, communicate with you regarding your purchase, and improve your experience with us. We do not sell your personal data to anyone.

Cookies

Our website may use minimal cookies to keep your shopping bag working smoothly during your visit. These contain no sensitive personal data.

Sharing Your Data

We share your delivery details only with our shipping partners to fulfil your order, and with payment providers to process your payment securely. We do not share your data with any other third parties except where required by law.

Data Retention

We retain order information as long as necessary to fulfil orders, handle any returns or exchanges, and meet legal or administrative requirements.

Your Rights

You may contact us at any time to ask what personal data we hold about you, or to request that we correct or erase it, subject to any records we are legally required to keep.
